📏 Form Standard

✅ Full lockout at the top — elbows fully extended

✅ Chest to fist-height at the bottom — no half-reps

✅ Straight line from head to heels — no sagging hips

✅ Steady rhythm from rep 1 — don't blast the first 40 and fail at 55

✅ Slow controlled breathing — a 100-rep set is a ~3-minute effort

❌ Count only clean reps — partial reps don't count toward 100
