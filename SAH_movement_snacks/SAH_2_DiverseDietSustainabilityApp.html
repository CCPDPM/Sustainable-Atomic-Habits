🔬 Protein Science

Plant Protein Bioavailability

DIAAS (Digestible Indispensable Amino Acid Score) is the gold-standard measure of protein quality. Score ≥ 1.0 = complete. Strategic combining raises scores significantly.

🔗 Protein Synergy & Bioavailability Boosters
